FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get access to the indicator after payment? ›
After payment, send your TradingView username to us on WhatsApp (+91 8629924099). We'll grant invite-only access within 24 hours. Then go to TradingView → Pine Script Indicators → Invite-Only Scripts and your indicator will be there, ready to add to any chart.
Do I need a paid TradingView account? ›
No! A free TradingView account works perfectly. You can create a free account at tradingview.com and our invite-only indicators will work on it. A paid TradingView plan gives you more indicators per chart, but is not required to use our tools.
Are profits guaranteed with these indicators? ›
No. SHI-SHA Tech indicators are AI-powered analysis tools, not profit generators. Trading always involves risk and there is no guarantee of profits. Signal accuracy is based on historical backtesting. Never invest more than you can afford to lose.
What is your refund policy? ›
No refunds are issued once TradingView invite access is granted, due to the digital nature of the product. We encourage you to contact our team on WhatsApp before purchasing if you have any questions about a specific indicator.
Can I use the indicator on mobile? ›
Yes! Since all indicators run on TradingView, you can access them on any device — desktop, laptop, tablet, or mobile — through the TradingView app or browser. Your invite access works across all your TradingView-connected devices.
Can I share my invite access with others? ›
No. Invite access is tied to your personal TradingView username and is strictly for personal use only. Sharing your account or invite access with others is a violation of our terms and will result in immediate revocation of access without refund.
What payment methods do you accept? ›
We accept UPI, Razorpay, Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay, Bank Transfer, and optionally Crypto. After placing your order via WhatsApp, our team will share payment details for your preferred method.
What markets do your indicators support? ›
Our indicators support NSE/BSE (Indian stock market), MCX (commodities — Crude Oil, Gold, Silver), Crypto (BTC, ETH, USDT pairs), Binary Options (OTC markets on Pocket Option, Quotex, Deriv), and Forex. Each combo is specifically designed for its market.
